2. No account, no cookies, no tracking

  • Tandem neither requires nor offers a user account and never asks for your name, email address or other contact details.

  • It contains no advertising and no analytics, tracking or profiling tools.

3. Hosting and delivery of the website

Tandem consists of static files only. They are stored on a virtual server that we rent from Hostinger (Hostinger International Ltd., 61 Lordou Vironos Street, 6023 Larnaca, Cyprus), located in Frankfurt, Germany, and delivered through the network of Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA): every request to tandem.linkandreas.de passes through Cloudflare, which provides the encrypted HTTPS connection, forwards the request to our server through a secure tunnel and protects it against attacks and overload.

When you open the website, your browser necessarily transmits technical information, such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page or file requested, the referring page and your browser type and version. This information is processed by Cloudflare and our server only to deliver the website and to keep it secure and stable (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is a secure, working website). The web server on our virtual server does not write access logs. Cloudflare processes connection data on our behalf and may also ask your browser to report failed connections to it (Network Error Logging). For the linkandreas.de zone, Cloudflare’s Email Obfuscation is also active: Cloudflare rewrites the email links on these pages so that automated programs cannot easily harvest the addresses, and a small Cloudflare script decodes them again in your browser. No personal data is processed beyond what is described above.

4. Data in your browser

Tandem processes everything you enter locally in your browser:

  • Group and rounds: The names you enter and the pairs of each round are saved in your browser’s local storage (localStorage), so that they are still there the next time you open Tandem.
  • Settings: Your language, your theme (light, dark or system) and whether you have already seen the welcome screen are saved there as well.
  • Example: The example uses made-up names; your own data stays untouched meanwhile.

None of this is transmitted to us or to third parties, and we cannot access it. Saving it in local storage is strictly necessary to provide the app you use (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). “Start over” removes the group and the rounds; you can delete everything else by clearing this website’s data in your browser’s settings.

The names you enter are personal data of the people concerned. As they are processed only in your browser and under your control, you decide which names to use – first names or initials are enough.

5. Files and clipboard

  • PDF and session file: The PDF with all rounds and the session file (JSON) are created in your browser and saved directly to your device. A session file you load is likewise read only in your browser.
  • Copying pairs: When you copy the pairs of a round, Tandem writes them to your device’s clipboard – only when you click.
